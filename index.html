// MANIFESTO.txt

THE PUNK PROGRAMMING MANIFESTO

  1. 01 IDEAS > IDE.

    Syntax was the gatekeeper. The compiler was the bouncer. We walk in through the side door, idea first.

  2. 02 SHIP UGLY, SHIP OFTEN.

    Your first demo should sound like a blown speaker in a basement. That's the point. Polish is for the second EP.

  3. 03 AI IS YOUR BAND, NOT YOUR BOSS.

    The model is the bassist. You're the one at the mic. Keep the vision yours, and read what it writes before you ship it.

  4. 04 THE SCENE > THE STACK.

    Share your weird. Trade riffs. Teach the kid in the back row. Nobody gatekeeps a chord progression.

  5. 05 DON'T ASK PERMISSION.

    Nobody licensed the first punk bands. You don't need a bootcamp, a résumé, or anyone's yes to build something.

  6. 06 HAVE A MESSAGE.

    Three chords are nothing without something to say. Build things that matter to someone, even if that someone is just you.

// HOUSE_RULES.txt

HOUSE RULES

Punk was never "anything goes." The best scenes looked out for each other. Here's ours.

  1. Punch up, never down. Mock bad tools, bloated stacks and gatekeeping. Never mock a person for being new.
  2. No dumb questions. Everyone started with three chords. If you know more, teach. Don't sneer.
  3. No hate. No harassment. Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, ableism: out the door, no warning.
  4. Credit your band. Say what tools and help you used. Credit the people whose work inspired you.
  5. Don't post secrets. No API keys, passwords, private data, or other people's personal info.
  6. No spam, no grift. Sharing your project is great. Ads, pyramid schemes and engagement bait are not.

See something that breaks these? Hit REPORT on the post. Enough reports and it comes down until a human checks it. Something urgent? Email info@3chords.dev.
