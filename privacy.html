PRIVACY, IN PLAIN WORDS

No ads. No trackers. No analytics. No third-party scripts, fonts or embeds. Here is everything we keep.

WHEN YOU POST ON THE WALL

We store the title, text, handle and flavor you enter, plus the time you posted. All of that is public. Don't post anything you wouldn't staple to a real wall.

Your browser gets a private "tear-down key" so only you can delete your post. It's kept in your browser's local storage. Clear your browser data and you lose the ability to delete it yourself, so ask us instead.

WHEN YOU SIGN THE WALL

Your handle is public. The optional "how to reach you" field is private: it is never shown on the site and never sold or shared. We only use it to tell you about the zine, open mics and jams.

SPAM AND ABUSE PROTECTION

To rate-limit spam and count one 🤘 per person, the server stores a salted, one-way hash of your IP address, never the IP itself. Reports on posts are counted the same way.

IN YOUR BROWSER

We use local storage (not cookies) for your handle, your tear-down keys, and whether the amp is switched on. It never leaves your device except the key, when you choose to tear down a post.

GETTING THINGS REMOVED

Tear down your own posts any time with the TEAR DOWN button. To remove a signature, your contact details, or a post you can't delete any more, email info@3chords.dev and tell us which one. We'll remove it.

QUESTIONS

Anything else about your data, or this page: info@3chords.dev. A human reads it.
